# C2 Full Content Authoring Prompt

Create original C2.1/C2.2 development content only for the exact lesson outlines supplied by the M16 C2 blueprint and the granular target allocation.

For every lesson:

1. Preserve its lesson ID, tier, unit, kind, prerequisites, target usage, and allowed exercise types.
2. Write two to four objectives and two to four concise teaching sections suitable for an independent C2 learner.
3. Provide three to six exercises with complete objective answers or analytic speaking/writing rubrics. Keep learner-facing prompts and answers distinct across the corpus.
4. Provide exactly four protocols: transcript-only listening with the transcript hidden before the first answer; bounded speaking; audio-dependent pronunciation with `NOT_ASSESSED` fallback; and draft-first writing feedback that never replaces the learner draft.
5. Use only allocated targets. Do not introduce external text, media, unsupported prerequisites, or later-level scope.
6. Mark content `PROJECT_AUTHORED` and leave its lifecycle `UNREVIEWED`.

Do not claim official CEFR endorsement, human review, release approval, provider authentication, live execution, real-media processing, learner outcomes, or public-distribution authority.
