# A2 pilot authored content

Create exactly ten original project-authored A2.1 lesson records for the first two units of the validated A2 blueprint.

Each record must include 2–4 learner-facing sections, 3–6 exercises with defensible answer data or a clear productive rubric, and exactly four protocols: listening, speaking, pronunciation, and writing.

Content scope is limited to recent events, event sequencing, follow-up questions, repair, quantities, shopping, polite requests, and local services. Use A2-appropriate language and concrete everyday contexts. Do not copy A1 lesson content or external material.

Protocol rules:

- Listening is transcript-only, with the transcript withheld until after the first answer and no required media.
- Speaking names learner and tutor roles, gives a concrete scenario and opening, asks a bounded follow-up, and ends after a clear completion condition.
- Pronunciation identifies an observable sound, stress, rhythm, or thought-group target and returns `NOT_ASSESSED` without learner audio.
- Writing requests a short learner draft and gives feedback only; it never replaces the draft.

Keep generated packets `PROJECT_AUTHORED` and `UNREVIEWED`. Do not include human-review, release, provider-authentication, live-AI, or public-distribution claims.
