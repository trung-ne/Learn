# A1 Pilot Content Authoring

Create original, concise A1.1 content for exactly these first ten M16 outline IDs:

1. `m16a1-v1-a1-u01-l01` through `m16a1-v1-a1-u01-l05`
2. `m16a1-v1-a1-u02-l01` through `m16a1-v1-a1-u02-l05`

Each authored record needs a title, 15–45 minute duration, objectives, two to four sections, three to six exercises, and exactly four prompts: listening, speaking, pronunciation, writing.

Keep explanations controlled and original. Objective exercises need answer data; speaking/writing exercises need rubrics. Listening text remains private until the learner answers. Pronunciation must report `NOT_ASSESSED` when no audio is supplied. Writing must request a draft and provide bounded feedback rather than a full replacement.

Do not add external text, media, provider claims, human-review fields, approval claims, or release language. Avoid copying the same learner-facing section, exercise instruction, or prompt into another lesson.
